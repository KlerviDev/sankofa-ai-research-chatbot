# Sankofa – AI Research Assistant

**An African-centred AI research assistant designed to support researchers throughout the research lifecycle.**

## 1. Overview

Sankofa is an AI-powered research assistant designed to help researchers manage and navigate their research projects, from the initial research idea to publication.

The platform aims to provide a structured workspace where researchers can organize their research activities, access relevant academic resources, and receive AI-powered guidance while maintaining ownership of their work.

Sankofa places African research contexts at the centre of its approach, promoting locally relevant knowledge, methodologies, policies, and academic contributions.

## 2. Problem Statement

Researchers often face challenges when managing the different stages of a research project, including:

- Finding and accessing relevant academic literature.
- Organizing research materials and documents.
- Developing research questions and methodologies.
- Managing data, analyses, and research drafts.
- Tracking tasks, deadlines, and project progress.
- Identifying reliable and contextually relevant information.

Existing research tools do not always adequately account for African research contexts, local institutional requirements, or the specific challenges faced by researchers in African countries.

Sankofa aims to address these challenges through an integrated, context-aware AI research assistant.

## 3. Objectives

The main objectives of Sankofa are to:

- Support researchers throughout the research lifecycle.
- Provide reliable, source-grounded answers using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
- Centralize research materials and project-related information.
- Maintain shared project context across multiple conversations.
- Promote African-led scholarship and locally relevant research practices.
- Support both English and French.
- Encourage responsible, ethical, and transparent use of AI in research.

## 4. Key Features

The planned features include:

### 4.1 Research Project Management
- Create and manage individual research workspaces.
- Organize research questions, literature, methodologies, datasets, analyses, and drafts.
- Track tasks, deadlines, and project progress.

### 4.2 AI-Powered Research Assistance
- Ask questions about research materials.
- Retrieve relevant information from uploaded documents and shared knowledge sources.
- Generate answers grounded in retrieved sources, with references and accessible links where available.
- Acknowledge uncertainty when sufficient evidence is unavailable.

### 4.3 Project Memory
- Maintain shared context across multiple conversations within the same research project.
- Enable researchers to continue their work without repeatedly providing the same background information.

### 4.4 African-Centred Research Support
- Consider the researcher's country, institution, and community context.
- Prioritize African scholarship, local policies, and relevant institutional guidelines.
- Identify potential mismatches between imported methodologies and local research contexts.

### 4.5 Multilingual Support
- Provide support in English and French.

## 5. Guiding Principles

Sankofa is developed around the following principles:

- **Researcher ownership:** The researcher remains the author and decision-maker. Sankofa acts as an assistant and coach.
- **Reliability:** AI-generated answers should be grounded in verifiable sources.
- **Privacy and security:** Research data must be handled responsibly, with appropriate access controls and consent.
- **Ethical research:** The platform should promote responsible data use, transparency, and respect for research ethics.
- **Accessibility:** The platform should consider low-bandwidth environments and accessibility standards.
- **African-centred approach:** African knowledge, perspectives, and research priorities should be represented and valued.
